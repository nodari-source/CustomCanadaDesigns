Embroidery is my obsession!

About

Our Embroidery as Art! You will love it!

My name is Liuba. I am Embroidery Designer, Digitizer, & Embroiderer. Embroidery is my obsession! In my ArtEmbroideryDesigns Shop I offer Instant Download Machine Embroidery Designs for embroidery lovers and business . I make a custom request and can do Custom Embroidery Digitizing (including business logo ) and Embroidery Service (direct embroidery and patches)!

I started embroidering in 2006. A couple of years I used my first embroidery machine and did a lot of embroidery in different techniques.Then I started to learn how to do embroidery digitizing. Nowdays I used professional embroidery Software Wilcom, industrial embroidery machine Tajima and do a lot of designs and stitch them. Embroidery is my obsession! Just see my designs in shop and you will see how much I love do that!
In 2013 I opened my etsy shop. My number one priority is customer satisfaction! So please let me know if we can do anything to help make your buying experience a better one. I welcome any ideas and suggestions or if you are looking for anything in particular contact me.
Thanks for stopping by and have a light stitches!
